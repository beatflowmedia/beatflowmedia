## Featured Project

### BeatFlow Music License App
**Role:** Lead Developer & Product Architect
**Timeline:** 2024 - Present
**Platform:** Web (beatflowmediagroup.com, studio.beatflowmediagroup.com)

#### Overview
BeatFlow is a comprehensive music licensing and studio services platform with dual-portal architecture serving both consumer and B2B markets. The platform combines a subscription-based music licensing service with custom sonic branding and audio production services, creating a unique hybrid business model in the competitive music licensing industry.

#### Business Model & Market Positioning

**Subscription Tiers:**
- **Student ($9.99/mo):** Unique in market - commercial licensing at entry price
- **Creator ($19.99/mo):** Unlimited channels, monetization enabled
- **Pro ($39.99/mo):** Broadcast rights, client work, $60 cheaper than competitors
- **Agency ($99/mo):** Team licensing + custom production services

**Studio Services (B2B):**
- Social Audio Kits: $250-$600 (custom sonic branding for social media)
- Mood Library: $29-$99 (curated ready-to-license collections)
- Invisible Services: Custom pricing (mixing, mastering, ghost production)

**Competitive Advantages:**
1. **Perpetual Licensing:** Download once, licensed forever (only Soundstripe offers this)
2. **Commercial Use at All Tiers:** Even $9.99 tier includes commercial licensing (unique in market)
3. **Hybrid Stock + Custom:** Only platform combining subscription library with custom production
4. **Transparent Artist Payments:** 50/50 revenue share with clear payout system
5. **Licensing Clarity:** No PRO complications, no hidden fees
