## Email 2: Detailed Explanation + FAQ (Day 7)
**Subject:** How BeatFlow's New Licensing Works (FAQ Inside)

**Timing:** 7 days after first email

```html
Hi [First Name],

Last week we announced updates to BeatFlow's licensing model. Here's a detailed breakdown of how it works:

**The New License Model:**

📹 **Published Content = Licensed Forever**
When you publish a video, podcast, or other content while subscribed, that specific project retains its license forever - even if you cancel later.

Example: You create a YouTube video using 3 BeatFlow tracks, publish it, then cancel your subscription. That video stays licensed. YouTube won't remove it, and you won't get copyright strikes.

🎵 **New Projects = Active Subscription Required**
After cancellation, you can't use your downloaded music in NEW projects. You'd need to reactivate your subscription.

Example: Same scenario - you cancelled after publishing your video. Now you want to create a NEW video. You'll need to reactivate your subscription to use BeatFlow music.

**Frequently Asked Questions:**

**Q: Do I need to do anything to protect my existing videos?**
A: No! All content you've already published is automatically protected forever.

**Q: Can I register my videos to make sure they're protected?**
A: Yes! We're launching a "Project Registration" dashboard where you can register your published content for extra peace of mind. (Coming soon)

**Q: What happens if I forget to register a video before cancelling?**
A: No problem! As long as the video was published DURING your active subscription, it stays licensed. Registration is just for your records.

**Q: Is this the same as Epidemic Sound?**
A: Yes! This is the exact same model. Epidemic Sound, Artlist, and other professional platforms all work this way.

**Q: Why can't I keep using downloaded tracks forever?**
A: Our previous model was unsustainable - users could pay $20 once and keep licenses forever. This new model ensures we can continue paying artists 70% revenue share and growing the catalog.

**Q: Can I "buy out" my licenses to make them permanent?**
A: Yes! We're introducing a "Perpetual Upgrade" option ($499/year) that converts all your downloads to lifetime licenses. Perfect for professional creators.

**Still have questions?**
Schedule a call with our team: [CALENDLY LINK]
Read full FAQ: [LINK]

Thank you for being part of BeatFlow!
- The BeatFlow Team
```

## Best Practices

**Subject Lines:**
- ✅ Clear, actionable
- ✅ Include dates when relevant
- ✅ Use emojis sparingly
- ❌ Don't oversell or be hyperbolic

**Email Body:**
- ✅ Lead with what matters to the user
- ✅ Use bullet points and formatting
- ✅ Clear CTAs (1-2 per email max)
- ✅ Always offer support contact
- ❌ Don't use corporate jargon

**Tone:**
- Transparent and honest
- Friendly but professional
- Empathetic to user concerns
- Solution-oriented
