## SWOT Summary (Quick Reference)

### STRENGTHS
1. Hybrid business model (stock + custom production) - unique in market
2. Competitive pricing ($9.99-$39.99) hits key segments
3. Perpetual licensing (if adopted) - major differentiator
4. Artist-friendly positioning potential

### WEAKNESSES
1. Small catalog (currently) - can't compete on volume yet
2. Brand awareness - competitors have massive marketing budgets
3. Catalog acquisition costs - need to license/produce thousands of tracks

### OPPORTUNITIES
1. Student market (millions of film students, underserved)
2. Creator economy explosion (38% unlicensed due to cost)
3. Wedding videography ($50B industry, recurring music needs)
4. Indie podcasters (millions of podcasts, growing)
5. AI hybrid models (human music + AI customization)
6. International expansion (emerging creator markets)

### THREATS
1. AI music platforms (Suno, Udio - cheap/free generation)
2. Subscription fatigue (consumers overwhelmed by monthly fees)
3. Price wars (competitors undercutting on price)
4. Platform direct licensing (YouTube/TikTok built-in libraries)
5. Copyright claim systems (false claims damage reputation)

## Critical Decision Points

### 1. LICENSING MODEL DECISION
**Question:** Perpetual per-download (Soundstripe) vs Published-while-active (Epidemic/Artlist)?

**Recommendation:** PERPETUAL
- Biggest marketing advantage
- Lowest customer friction
- Highest retention (no fear of losing licenses)
- Only Soundstripe does this currently

### 2. PRO/COPYRIGHT STRATEGY
**Question:** Own catalog rights (Epidemic) vs Manage rights (Musicbed/Artlist)?

**Recommendation:** OWN RIGHTS if possible
- Eliminates PRO complications (Artlist's #1 complaint)
- Simpler customer experience
- Stronger legal position (no Content ID claims)
- Higher upfront costs but cleaner long-term

### 3. CATALOG SIZE STRATEGY
**Question:** Volume (AudioJungle 1.3M) vs Curation (PremiumBeat 30k)?

**Recommendation:** CURATED QUALITY
- Can't compete with AudioJungle/Pond5 on size
- Position like PremiumBeat/Musicbed (boutique, high-quality)
- Target: 5,000-10,000 curated tracks Year 1
- Focus on use-case organization (TikTok music, wedding music, podcast intros)

### 4. ARTIST PAYMENT MODEL
**Question:** Upfront fees (Artlist) vs Revenue share (Musicbed) vs Hybrid?

**Recommendation:** HYBRID
- Small upfront fee ($50-$200/track acceptance)
- Plus ongoing revenue share (50/50 split of tier revenue attributed to usage)
- Attracts artists (upfront payment) + incentivizes quality (rev share)
- Transparent, marketable as "artist-friendly"

### 5. CHANNEL LIMIT STRATEGY
**Question:** Limit channels like Epidemic/Artlist (1-3 per tier) or unlimited?

**Recommendation:** UNLIMITED at Creator tier and above
- Competitive advantage over Epidemic/Artlist
- Lower customer friction
- Simpler to communicate
- Student tier: 1 channel (prevents abuse)
- Creator+: Unlimited channels
